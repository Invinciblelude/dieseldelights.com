## How We Split It

### Option A — Revenue Share (Recommended)
| Party | Gets |
|-------|------|
| **You** | 60–70% of all Diesel Delights sales |
| **Me** | 30–40% of all Diesel Delights sales |
| **You also keep** | All incremental drink/pho sales from new foot traffic I drive |

### Option B — Flat Kitchen Fee
| Party | Gets |
|-------|------|
| **You** | Flat $1,500–$2,500/month kitchen fee + % of delivery sales |
| **Me** | All Diesel Delights revenue minus fee and food costs |

### Option C — Pop-Up Test First
| Party | Gets |
|-------|------|
| **You** | Zero risk — I run 2–3 Friday/Saturday pop-up nights |
| **Me** | Proof of concept, real sales data, customer feedback |
| **Then** | We decide on Option A or B based on results |

## Why This Works For You

1. **Zero risk** — Your current menu doesn't change. I add revenue on top.
2. **No capital needed** — I supply recipes, branding, sauces, and marketing.
3. **New customers** — My marketing brings people who've never been to your shop.
4. **Higher ticket** — Your pho customers add Diesel Delights snacks. $12 check becomes $18–$22.
5. **Delivery revenue** — Diesel Delights on apps means orders flowing to YOUR kitchen address.
6. **Off-peak activation** — Dead hours between lunch and dinner? That's when Diesel Delights runs.
