# Diesel Delights — Collab / Ghost Kitchen Business Plan

> Low-capital launch strategy: partner with existing pho restaurants
> to validate and grow the Diesel Delights brand.

---

## Why Collab First

| Own Restaurant | Collab Model |
|---------------|-------------|
| $30K–$80K buildout | $0–$2K startup |
| 6–12 month lease commitment | 90-day test, walk away anytime |
| Full staffing + payroll | Use their existing staff |
| All risk on you | Risk shared with partner |
| Permits, inspections, insurance | Partner's existing permits cover you |
| Months to open | Live in 2 weeks |

---

## The 3-Phase Strategy

### Phase 1 — Pop-Up Proof (Weeks 1–4)
**Goal:** Prove demand with real sales data

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Target 3–5 independent pho shops | Carmichael, Natomas, Midtown, Elk Grove |
| Offer free pop-up nights | Friday + Saturday, 5–9 PM |
| You cook, they host | Bring your own sauces + ingredients |
| Track everything | Plates sold, revenue, customer feedback |
| Cost to you | ~$200–$400 in ingredients per pop-up |

**Success metric:** 25+ plates sold per pop-up night = move to Phase 2.

### Phase 2 — Kitchen Collab (Months 2–4)
**Goal:** Run Diesel Delights as a permanent add-on menu

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Sign rev-share deal with best partner | 60/40 or 65/35 (them/you) |
| Train their kitchen on all 7 dishes + 5 sauces | 2–3 training sessions |
| Launch full menu in-house | Branded section, table tents, counter signage |
| Launch on DoorDash + Uber Eats | "Diesel Delights at [Partner Name]" |
| Run social media hard | Post daily, tag location, drive new customers |
| Add Viet drip coffee + canned drinks | High-margin, zero-prep add-ons |

**Success metric:** $8K+/month in Diesel Delights sales = move to Phase 3.

### Phase 3 — Scale or Own (Months 5–12)
**Goal:** Use collab profits + data to decide next move

| Path | When |
|------|------|
| **Expand to 2–3 partner kitchens** | If demand is high + partner model works |
| **Ghost kitchen on delivery apps** | If delivery outperforms dine-in |
| **Open your own spot** | When you've saved $20K+ and have proven sales data |
| **License the brand** | If other restaurants want to run Diesel Delights |

## Target Partner Profile

**Ideal pho restaurant partner:**

| Criteria | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Independent (not a chain) | Easier to negotiate, faster decisions |
| Vietnamese-owned | Cultural alignment, they understand pho/sauces |
| Visible location with parking | Fair Oaks Blvd, Stockton Blvd, Florin Rd corridors |
| Has slow periods (2–5 PM, weeknight dinners) | That's when Diesel Delights activates |
| Open to new ideas | Some owners are hungry for extra revenue |
| Kitchen has wok + grill access | Needed for fried rice, tri-tip, oysters |

| Restaurant | Location | Why |

## Financial Model — Collab

### Startup Costs

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Sauce ingredients (first batch) | $150 |
| Branded materials (table tents, stickers, packaging) | $200–$500 |
| Phin filters + coffee supplies | $100 |
| Canned drink inventory (first order) | $200 |
| Marketing/social media | $0 (you do it) |
| **Total startup** | **$650–$950** |

### Monthly P&L — Collab at One Location

*Based on 30 orders/day, $15 avg check*

| Line Item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Monthly Revenue** | $13,500 |
| Food Cost (30%) | ($4,050) |
| **Gross Profit** | $9,450 |
| Partner's Cut (65%) | ($6,143) |
| **Your Cut (35%)** | **$3,308** |
| Your Sauce/Coffee Costs | (~$300) |
| Marketing/Materials | (~$100) |
| **Your Net Profit** | **~$2,900/month** |

### Scale to 3 Locations

| Metric | 1 Location | 3 Locations |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Monthly Revenue | $13,500 | $40,500 |
| Your Cut (35%) | $3,308 | $9,923 |
| Your Net Profit | ~$2,900 | ~$8,700 |
| Annual Net | ~$34,800 | ~$104,400 |

At 3 locations you're making **$100K+/year** with almost no overhead, no lease,
and no employees. That's your runway to open your own spot.

## Ghost Kitchen / Delivery-Only Add-On

Run alongside the collab — extra revenue from apps.

| Platform | Commission | Your Effective Margin |
|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| DoorDash | 15–30% | Still profitable at $10 plates |
| Uber Eats | 15–30% | Drinks are pure profit even after commission |
| Direct orders (website) | 0% | Build this over time |

**Ghost kitchen play:** List "Diesel Delights" as a separate restaurant on
delivery apps, operating from your partner's kitchen. Customers order Diesel
Delights on DoorDash; their kitchen cooks it; you split revenue.

## The Pitch Approach

### How to walk into a pho restaurant:

1. **Go eat there first.** Order their food. Be a real customer.
2. **Talk to the owner casually.** Compliment the food. Mention you're a chef.
3. **Ask for 15 minutes of their time.** Not during rush hour.
4. **Bring printed materials:** Your one-page pitch + photos from dieseldelights.com.
5. **Bring sauce samples.** Let them taste DC, DFG, DH. The sauces sell themselves.
6. **Propose the pop-up test.** "Let me do 2 Friday nights. If it works, we talk numbers."
7. **No pressure.** If they say no, thank them and move to the next one.

### What to say:

> "I'm Vince — I'm a firefighter and chef building a Vietnamese-American snack
> brand called Diesel Delights. I've got 7 dishes and 5 house sauces that would
> work great alongside your menu. I'm looking for a kitchen partner — I handle
> all the marketing, branding, and recipes. You keep 60–65% of every Diesel
> Delights sale. Can I show you what I've got?"

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Partner cooks your dishes poorly | Detailed recipe cards + training sessions + taste checks |
| Partner wants to steal your recipes | Keep sauce recipes proprietary — you make sauces off-site |
| Low sales in first month | Start with pop-ups to test before committing |
| Partner relationship goes bad | 90-day exit clause in any agreement |
| Delivery commissions eat profit | Focus on dine-in collab first, add delivery as bonus |
| Can't find willing partner | Approach 5+ restaurants — you only need 1 yes |
