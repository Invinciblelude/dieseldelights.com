## Verdict

**Strong fit.** High-traffic corridor, demographics match the $10 value concept, Mexican
competition has cleared out, and the Asian fusion gap is wide open. Drinks push margins
well above industry average. At 95 customers/day (very achievable for Fair Oaks Blvd traffic),
Diesel Delights nets ~$13K/month profit with room to grow.
